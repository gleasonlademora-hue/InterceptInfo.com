Comprehensive Privacy Policy

1. Introduction to Our Privacy Practices

Welcome to InterceptInfo. Much like the careful engineering required to build a Storm Intercept Vehicle capable of withstanding two hundred mile-per-hour winds, we believe in building robust digital infrastructure to protect your personal information. We are committed to maintaining the trust of our users, atmospheric science enthusiasts, and generous donors. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, tracking, and sharing of your personal data when you visit our website, interact with our active and inactive vehicle database, or contribute to our mission. Please read this document carefully to understand how your digital presence is handled within our ecosystem.

2. What Data We Collect

To provide a seamless and informative experience, InterceptInfo collects both personal and non-personal data. We explicitly collect personal information such as your names and email addresses when you voluntarily provide them. This occurs primarily via our newsletter signup prompts, account registration pages, and our donation submission forms. When you support the maintenance of vehicle armor or the purchase of scientific probes, we also collect necessary billing information. However, please note that all sensitive financial data is processed securely through encrypted third-party payment gateways, and we do not store full credit card numbers on our local servers. Furthermore, if you reach out to us via our contact forms with questions regarding vehicle history or engineering specifications, we retain a record of that correspondence to better serve you in the future.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Just as meteorologists use radar to track the path of a supercell, we utilize digital tracking technologies to monitor the flow of traffic on our website. We clarify that we use cookies, web beacons, and external analytics platforms most notably Google Analytics to track how users browse our infographics, interact with our interactive history carousels, and navigate between the active and inactive vehicle tabs. These cookies collect non-identifiable data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and the duration of your visit. This tracking is strictly analytical. By understanding which vehicle blueprints or technical specifications receive the most attention, we can tailor our future content to better match the interests of our community. You reserve the right to disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may limit your ability to use dynamic features like saving your preferred system theme.

4. Exactly How Your Data is Used

We believe in complete transparency regarding the utilization of your digital footprint. We state exactly why we need the data: primarily, it is used to send updates and to optimize website performance. If you have provided your email address, we use it to send periodic newsletters containing severe weather deployment updates, structural changes to vehicles like the Dominator 3, and updates on how your donations are actively funding scientific research in Tornado Alley. Analytically, the data gathered via cookies is used to optimize website performance, ensuring that our high-resolution imagery and complex data arrays load efficiently regardless of the device you are using. We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling outside of the basic context of enhancing user experience.

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

InterceptInfo operates on a strict ethical standard. We explicitly state that we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal user data with aggressive marketers or external advertisers. Your privacy is not a commodity to us. However, in order to operate a modern, functional website, we do share limited, necessary data with trusted third-party service providers. This includes sharing information with our secure hosting providers to maintain server uptime, external tools like Google Analytics to process broad traffic trends, and verified payment gateways (such as Stripe or PayPal) to safely facilitate your generous donations. These third parties are contractually obligated to keep your information confidential and are prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing their specific service to InterceptInfo.

6. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

We believe you should have absolute control over your personal information. In accordance with global privacy standards, we include clear instructions on your user rights. Visitors can request full access to the specific personal data we hold about them. If you discover that your name, email, or contact information is inaccurate, you have the right to request that we edit and correct it immediately. Furthermore, you possess the right to be forgotten; you can request that we entirely delete your personal data from our active databases and mailing lists at any time. To execute any of these rights, simply contact our administration team using the details provided below, and we will process your request within thirty business days.

7. Contact Information for Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please do not hesitate to reach out. We provide a dedicated email address and point of contact for all privacy-related inquiries.
