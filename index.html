Vehicle Overview & History

The Tornado Intercept Vehicle 1 (TIV-1) was constructed by Sean Casey in 2003. Built upon a 1997 Ford F-450 Super Duty chassis, it featured heavy steel armor and bullet-resistant windows designed to withstand the violent debris of a low-level tornado. Its primary mission was to carry an IMAX camera directly into the path of a twister for documentary filmmaking.

Unique Traits & Features
  • 360-degree rotating turret housing a custom IMAX film camera setup.
  • Rear drop-down protective rubber/steel skirts to prevent undercarriage wind lift.
  • Heavy-duty leaf spring reinforcement to support the massive steel frame.

Vehicle Overview & History

Addressing the weight and top-speed issues of TIV-1, TIV-2 was engineered on a Dodge Ram 3500 chassis. It features a sophisticated hydraulic skirt that drops to prevent wind from lifting the undercarriage, along with ground-penetrating steel spikes. TIV-2 successfully filmed inside a violent tornado in 2013, validating its robust aerodynamic and anchoring systems.

Unique Traits & Features
  • Hydraulic ground-penetrating spikes (40 inches long) to anchor into the soil.
  • Full 4-side hydraulic skirts that seal completely to the ground surface.
  • Custom 6-wheel-drive configuration for enhanced mud and off-road traction.

Vehicle Overview & History

Created by Reed Timmer, Dominator 1 took a different approach by focusing strictly on aerodynamics rather than pure weight and anchoring. Built from a 2007 Chevrolet Tahoe, it utilized tough Lexan polycarbonate armor and a sloped outer shell. This design forces oncoming winds over the vehicle, effectively pinning it to the ground during a severe storm.

Unique Traits & Features
  • Custom Lexan windshield capable of withstanding high-velocity hail impact.
  • Airbags/hydraulic lowering system that drops the front body to block under-chassis wind.
  • Rhino/Line-X exterior protective spray coating against debris scour.

Vehicle Overview & History

The third iteration in the Dominator series was built on a massive heavy-duty Ford F-350 chassis. It refined the aerodynamic principles of its predecessors while adding advanced features like automated gull-wing doors and an air suspension system. When fully deployed, the entire vehicle lowers flush to the ground, eliminating underbody wind flow completely.

Unique Traits & Features
  • Automated hydraulic gull-wing passenger doors for quick equipment access.
  • Air suspension drop system that lowers the vehicle chassis flush to the ground.
  • Dual hydraulic ground spikes that stake deep into asphalt/soil for extreme anchoring.
