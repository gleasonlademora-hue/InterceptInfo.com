Vehicle Overview & History

The Tornado Intercept Vehicle 1 (TIV-1) was constructed by Sean Casey in 2003. Built upon a 1997 Ford F-450 Super Duty chassis, it featured heavy steel armor and bullet-resistant windows designed to withstand the violent debris of a low-level tornado.

Unique Traits & Features
  • 360-degree rotating turret housing a custom IMAX film camera setup.
  • Rear drop-down protective rubber/steel skirts to prevent undercarriage wind lift.
  • Heavy-duty leaf spring reinforcement to support the massive steel frame.

  • Armor Material: Steel tubing skin, composite armour & 1.63 inch Lexan
Vehicle Overview & History

Addressing the weight and top-speed issues of TIV-1, TIV-2 was engineered on a Dodge Ram 3500 chassis. It features a hydraulic skirt that drops to prevent wind lift along with ground-penetrating steel spikes.

Unique Traits & Features
  • Hydraulic ground-penetrating spikes (40 inches long) to anchor into soil.
  • Full 4-side hydraulic skirts that seal completely to the ground.
  • Custom 6-wheel-drive configuration.

Vehicle Overview & History

Created by Reed Timmer, Dominator 1 focused on aerodynamics rather than pure weight. Built from a 2007 Chevrolet Tahoe, it utilized Lexan polycarbonate armor and a sloped outer shell.

Unique Traits & Features
  • Custom Lexan windshield capable of withstanding high-velocity hail.
  • Airbags/hydraulic lowering system that drops the front body.
  • Line-X exterior protective spray coating.
